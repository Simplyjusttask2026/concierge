6. Double-click agent-laptop/install.bat (installs Python agent + browser).
7. Double-click agent-laptop/login_nextdoor.bat and log into your PERSONAL Nextdoor account yourself.
8. Bookmark: https://nextdoor.com/news_feed/ , https://nextdoor.com/notifications/ , https://nextdoor.com/inbox/

10. Right-click agent-laptop/install_schedule.ps1 > Run with PowerShell (every 2h, 7am-10pm).
Control Panel > Power Options > closing lid = Do nothing when plugged in.
